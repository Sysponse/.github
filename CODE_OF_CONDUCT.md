# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

At Sysponse, we are committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and collaborative work environment. As a team dedicated to building life-saving emergency management software, we hold ourselves to the highest standards of conduct and professionalism.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

Team members are expected to:

- ✅ **Be Professional**: Maintain professionalism in all communications and interactions
- ✅ **Be Respectful**: Treat all team members with respect and courtesy
- ✅ **Be Collaborative**: Work together effectively and support team goals
- ✅ **Be Constructive**: Provide helpful, constructive feedback in code reviews and discussions
- ✅ **Be Accountable**: Take ownership of your work and commitments
- ✅ **Be Inclusive**: Value diverse perspectives and create an welcoming environment
- ✅ **Be Solution-Oriented**: Focus on solving problems rather than placing blame
- ✅ **Communicate Clearly**: Share information transparently and in a timely manner

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are unacceptable:

- ❌ Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments
- ❌ Personal attacks or insults
- ❌ Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- ❌ Sharing confidential or proprietary information inappropriately
- ❌ Unprofessional conduct in any team setting
- ❌ Any behavior that creates an unsafe or uncomfortable work environment

---

## Code Quality and Technical Standards

### Professional Development Practices

- Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code
- Follow established coding standards and conventions
- Participate constructively in code reviews
- Test your code thoroughly before submitting
- Be open to feedback and willing to learn
- Share knowledge and help teammates grow

### Code Review Guidelines

When reviewing code:
- Focus on the code, not the person
- Ask questions rather than making demands
- Provide specific, actionable feedback
- Acknowledge good work
- Be respectful of different approaches and styles

When receiving reviews:
- Be open to feedback
- Ask for clarification when needed
- Respond professionally to comments
- View reviews as learning opportunities
- Don't take criticism personally

---

## Security and Confidentiality

- **Protect sensitive information**: Never share credentials, API keys, or sensitive data
- **Follow security protocols**: Adhere to all security policies and procedures
- **Report vulnerabilities responsibly**: Use proper channels to report security issues
- **Respect data privacy**: Handle user and system data with appropriate care
- **Maintain confidentiality**: Keep proprietary information confidential

---

## Communication Standards

### Team Communication

- Be responsive to messages and requests
- Use appropriate channels for different types of communication
- Keep team members informed of your progress and blockers
- Attend scheduled meetings and be prepared
- Be mindful of time zones and work schedules

### Documentation

- Document your code and architectural decisions
- Keep technical documentation up to date
- Write clear commit messages and PR descriptions
- Share knowledge through documentation

---

## Conflict Resolution

Disagreements and conflicts are normal in any team. When conflicts arise:

1. **Address issues directly**: Speak with the person involved first
2. **Focus on the issue, not personalities**: Keep discussions professional
3. **Seek to understand**: Listen to different perspectives
4. **Find common ground**: Work toward mutually acceptable solutions
5. **Escalate appropriately**: If direct resolution fails, involve team leads or management

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. **Document the incident**: Note what happened, when, and who was involved
2. **Report to your manager or team lead**: Use appropriate reporting channels
3. **Maintain confidentiality**: Protect privacy during the reporting process
4. **Cooperate with investigations**: Provide information as needed

All reports will be taken seriously and handled confidentially.

---

## Consequences

Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in:

- Verbal or written warning
- Mandatory training or coaching
- Temporary suspension of privileges
- Termination of employment (for serious or repeated violations)

The specific consequences will depend on the severity and frequency of the violation.

---

## Our Mission-Critical Responsibility

As developers of emergency management software, our code and systems directly impact public safety and emergency response. We must:

- **Prioritize reliability**: Build systems that first responders can depend on
- **Maintain high quality**: Lives may depend on our software working correctly
- **Act with integrity**: Make decisions that prioritize user safety and system reliability
- **Stay vigilant**: Be proactive about quality, security, and performance

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All GitHub repositories and activities
- Team meetings and communications
- Code reviews and technical discussions
- Any setting where you represent Sysponse
- All work-related interactions, whether in-person or virtual
